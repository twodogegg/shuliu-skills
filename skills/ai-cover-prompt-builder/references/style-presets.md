# Style Presets

Use these presets as direction, not rigid recipes.

## short-drama-poster

适用于 9:16 中文微短剧海报。用前、中、后景的人物层次先讲清关系和冲突：主角占据前景或视觉中心，配角以对视、背离、守护或对峙形成戏剧张力。主标题占画面约四分之一，少而重，按题材选择明确色彩和材质：都市情感用暖金、奶油白与电影柔光；逆袭玄幻用金色书法、火焰和能量环；规则悬疑用黑、灰、白、警示红与破损字；赛车热血用高饱和红黑、金属质感和速度线。详细构图与模板见 `short-drama-poster.md`。

适用：都市情感、豪门逆袭、重生穿越、玄幻爽剧、规则怪谈、复仇悬疑、赛车热血。

## clean-tech-tutorial

A polished AI tutorial thumbnail. Use a dark tech workspace, sharp laptop or app UI in the background, a right-side creator portrait, medium-large title text, and a black, purple, orange, white, and yellow palette. Keep the layout clean and readable.

Best for: AI tools, practical tutorials, beginner guides, and evergreen explainers.

## notion-workspace

A Notion-flavored productivity thumbnail. Use a visible Notion-like page, dashboard, database, board, or second-brain workspace. Keep the layout organized, product-led, and clean. Use black, white, gray, muted purple, and one accent color.

Best for: Notion workflows, AI knowledge bases, SOP systems, productivity dashboards, and second-brain topics.

## dopamine-absurd-clickbait

A loud high-click YouTube thumbnail. Use yellow, black, red, and white with exaggerated contrast, comic burst labels, warning accents, arrows, lightning, big numbers, and a shocked or expressive creator. Keep it absurd and energetic, but still readable.

Best for: hot takes, AI tricks, mistake/avoidance topics, dramatic claims, and tool roundups.

## viral-neon-ai-tools

An energetic AI tools thumbnail. Use deep black and purple with cyan, blue, and yellow glow accents. Include app UI cards and 1 to 3 app icons. The creator can point toward the title or tool interface.

Best for: AI tools comparison, prompt tricks, agent workflows, and tool discovery videos.

## minimalist-premium-tech

A restrained premium thumbnail. Use matte black, gray, white, and one refined accent color. Keep text smaller, spacing wider, and effects minimal. Use a single polished product screen or dashboard.

Best for: professional content, strategy videos, high-end tech analysis, and brand-focused covers.
